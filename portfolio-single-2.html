AagesLend

Project Date April 2026
Role Smart Contract Architecture, DeFi
Github 0xAnandDev/AagesLend
Visit Live aageslend-finance.app

AagesLend is a production-grade decentralized lending protocol engineered to enable secure, non-custodial capital markets on EVM-compatible chains. The protocol implements over-collateralized lending vaults, dynamic interest rate curves, robust role-based access control (RBAC), and a bulletproof health factor liquidation engine to safeguard depositors' liquidity.

During development, strict attention was paid to gas optimization strategies, successfully implementing assembly-level storage packing, efficient loops, and memory caching to achieve sub-100k gas execution costs on core lending and repayment transactions. The resulting system demonstrates DeFi architecture at its peak performance and safety standards.
